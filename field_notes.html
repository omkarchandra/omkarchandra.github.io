Electrophysiology Field Notes — Expanded Study Guide

PDF · 57 pages · based on sessions from Sep 10 & Sep 21, 2026

A study guide covering patch-clamp electrophysiology from the bench up: brain-slice preparation and rig basics, aCSF and the Nernst equation, synaptic plasticity, cell-attached recording, and Cs⁺-based internal solutions. It was built directly on 31 pages of handwritten notes taken across three lab sessions — an undated slice-prep/rig session, cultured neurons with astrocyte co-cultures (Sep 10, 2026), and aCSF, Nernst, plasticity, cell-attached and Cs⁺ internals (Sep 21, 2026).

How it was created

  • The handwritten notebook pages were scanned to PDF and given to Claude (Opus 5.5) for expansion.
  • Every page was transcribed and kept in the notebook's original order, with its original headings and flow.
  • Each entry was then expanded with background, context, and the underlying physics — written to teach the fundamentals from first principles.
  • 19 figures were simulated from standard models (a Hodgkin–Huxley action potential, clamp protocols, I–V curves, Nernst calculations), with consistent colour coding throughout (K⁺ blue, Na⁺ orange, and so on). None of the figures use real data.
  • 9 animations are embedded in the PDF (they play in Adobe Acrobat Reader or Okular), and are also included below as videos that play in any browser.
  • A "dig deeper" note in the margin of the Cs⁺ internal-solution page became the guide's longest section: why Cs⁺ is used, gluconate vs chloride, separating EPSCs from IPSCs by holding potential, a typical recipe, and pitfalls.
  • A box in the cerebellum section links Purkinje-cell electrophysiology to the SCA1 literature.
  • Ambiguous handwriting and values that looked off were flagged rather than silently changed — all listed in Appendix C.
  • The final guide was typeset in LaTeX.
